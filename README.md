
# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed a Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard to analyze retail sales performance, order trends, product demand, store locations, and revenue by product size.

The dashboard provides insights into sales by day of the week and hour of the day, top-selling products, store performance, and product-level revenue to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and order volume.
- Understand average bill per person and average order quantity.
- Identify sales trends by day of the week and hour of the day.
- Compare sales performance across store locations.
- Identify top-selling products by quantity.
- Analyze revenue by product type and product size.
- Identify opportunities to improve inventory planning and store operations.

## 🔍 Key Questions Answered

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were recorded?
- What is the average bill per person?
- What is the average order quantity?
- Which days of the week have the highest order volumes?
- During which hours are order volumes highest?
- Which store locations have the highest order volumes?

## 💡 Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

- Total revenue is approximately **$698K**.
- The dashboard reports **149.12K total orders**.
- Average bill per person is **$4.68**.
- Average order quantity is **1.44**.

### 2. Sales by Day of the Week

- Friday and Thursday have the highest displayed order counts, at approximately **21.7K each**.
- Monday follows at approximately **21.6K**.
- Saturday has the lowest displayed order count, at approximately **20.5K**.

These trends can help with staffing and inventory planning across different days.

### 3. Sales by Hour of the Day

- The highest displayed order count occurs at approximately **10 AM**, with around **19K orders**.
- Order activity is also high between 8 AM and 9 AM, at approximately 18K.
- Order counts decline after 10 AM, reaching approximately 1K by 8 PM.

### 4. Store Location Analysis

The dashboard compares three store locations:

| Store Location | Approximate Order Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Hell's Kitchen | 51K |
| Astoria | 51K |
| Lower Manhattan | 48K |

Hell's Kitchen and Astoria have the highest displayed order quantities, followed by Lower Manhattan.

### 5. Top-Selling Products by Quantity

The dashboard highlights the following leading products:

### 6. Revenue by Product Size

| Product Size | Approximate Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Large | $239K | 34.25% |
| Regular | $194K | 27.82% |

Large-size products account for the highest displayed revenue share, at approximately **34.25%**.

The “Not Defined” category also contributes a substantial share of revenue and should be investigated to understand whether product-size information is missing or not applicable.
