
# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

### 📊 Retail Sales | Product Performance | Customer Purchasing Trends

An end-to-end retail sales analysis project focused on understanding coffee shop performance through sales trends, product analysis, store comparisons, and peak sales periods. The project aims to uncover actionable insights that can help improve business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The main objective of this project is to analyse retail sales data to gain actionable insights that can enhance the performance of a coffee shop.

The analysis explores sales patterns by day and hour, monthly revenue, store location performance, product popularity, product categories, and order value.

By transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights, this project helps identify high-performing products, busy sales periods, and opportunities to improve store operations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse sales performance by day of the week and hour of the day.
- Identify peak sales periods and customer purchasing patterns.
- Calculate monthly sales revenue.
- Compare sales performance across store locations.
- Analyse average bill per person and average order quantity.
- Identify best-selling products by quantity and revenue.
- Compare revenue across product categories and product types.
- Generate insights to support business decisions.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Conclusions

### 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total revenue is approximately **$698K**.
- Total orders are approximately **149.12K**.
- Average bill per person is **$4.68**.
- Average order quantity is **1.44**.

### 2. Top-Selling Products by Quantity

The top five products by quantity sold are:

| Rank | Product | Quantity Sold |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Brewed Coffee | 23K |
| 2 | Gourmet Brew | 17K |
| 3 | Barista Espresso | 17K |
| 4 | Hot Chocolate | 11K |
| 5 | Brewed Black | 11K |

These products represent important contributors to product demand and can help inform inventory planning.

### 3. Revenue by Product Size

| Product Size | Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Not Defined | $208K | 29.8% |
| Large | $194K | 27.8% |
| Regular | $173K | 24.7% |
| Small | $122K | 17.5% |

- Large-size products contribute approximately **$194K**, or 27.8% of revenue.
- The “Not Defined” category contributes the largest reported amount, at $208K.
- The undefined-size category should be investigated to determine whether product size information is missing or not applicable.

### 4. Peak Sales Times

**By day of the week**
- Monday and Tuesday record the highest reported order volumes, at approximately 22K each.
- Sunday records the lowest reported order volume, at approximately 16K.

**By hour of the day**
- Sales peak at approximately **10 AM**, with a reported 18.9K orders.
- Activity tapers off sharply after 12 PM.

These patterns can help with staff scheduling, stock preparation, and operational planning.

### 5. Top Store Locations by Sales

The leading store locations identified in the analysis are:

- **Hell's Kitchen**
- **Astoria**
- **Lower Manhattan**

All three locations are reported to perform similarly in sales volume. Further analysis of costs and profit margins would be needed to determine which store is most profitable.
